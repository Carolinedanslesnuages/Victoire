# Privacy Policy for PACE

**Last Updated: November 8, 2024**

## Introduction

PACE ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

When you use PACE, you may provide:
- Budget configuration data (monthly income, fixed charges, savings goals, budget cycle start day)
- Expense records (amount, description, category, date)
- User preferences (currency, custom categories)
- Subscription information (managed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store)

### Automatically Collected Information

We do not automatically collect any personal information or tracking data. PACE does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

## How We Store Your Information

All your data is stored **locally on your device only**. We do not:
- Transmit your data to external servers
- Store your data in the cloud (unless you explicitly use a cloud backup feature)
- Share your data with third parties
- Use your data for advertising or analytics

Your budget data, expenses, and preferences remain private and under your control on your device.

## Subscription Data

If you purchase a PACE Premium subscription:
- The subscription is processed and managed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store
- We only receive confirmation of your subscription status through the respective platform's API
- Your payment information is handled exclusively by Apple/Google and is not accessible to us

## Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you:
- Delete the app
- Use the "Reset Application" feature within the app settings
- Clear the app's data through your device settings

## Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:
- **Access**: All your data is visible within the app
- **Modification**: You can edit or delete any expense or configuration at any time
- **Deletion**: You can reset all data using the "Reset Application" feature in Settings
- **Export**: Premium users can export their data (CSV, PDF)

## Children's Privacy

PACE is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Third-Party Services

PACE uses the following third-party services:
- **React Native Purchases**: For managing in-app subscriptions (Apple App Store / Google Play Store)
  - Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  - Subject to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data:
- All data is stored locally using secure storage mechanisms provided by iOS and Android
- No data is transmitted over the network (except subscription verification with app stores)

## International Users

PACE is designed to be used globally. Since all data is stored locally on your device, no data transfers across borders occur.
